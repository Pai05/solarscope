How to scan
  1. Best: stand on the terrace. Or walk around the house outside.
  2. Move the phone slowly over the floor until a white ring appears.
  3. Point the ring at each corner (where the wall meets the floor or ground) and tap, in order around the roof.
  4. Tap Close shape. Then Add obstruction and tap around each water tank or stair room.
  5. Tap Finish. Daylight and slow movement give the best accuracy.
